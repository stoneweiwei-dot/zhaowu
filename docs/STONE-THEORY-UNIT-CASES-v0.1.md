## Purpose

These cases test whether the runtime can apply a few recurring operational ideas without turning them into a second master method.

Canonical route:

**宮位／場域 → 十神功能 → 根路與做功 → 作用結果 → 歲運應期 → 可信度**

The source itself presents a layered route of 理法 → 象法 → 技法 → 歲運應期, and repeatedly warns against reading a single 十神、合沖或字面象 as a complete verdict.

## 12 synthetic cases

1. **TUC-01 宮位優先** — same Ten God in different palaces must not collapse into one event.
2. **TUC-02 虛實與根氣** — visible but rootless/blocked is not equal to rooted and effective.
3. **TUC-03 賓主與體用** — external resources become usable only after an effective relation with the subject and capacity.
4. **TUC-04 合來／合絆／合閉** — distinguish function of combination before judging outcome.
5. **TUC-05 沖庫真假** — collision does not automatically open a storehouse.
6. **TUC-06 婚姻宮星歲運鏈** — relationship events require palace/star/operation/timing alignment.
7. **TUC-07 遷移與職業變動** — distinguish home, workplace and distant-field activation.
8. **TUC-08 傷病災三級觸發** — natal basis → luck-stage support → annual trigger; never a medical diagnosis.
9. **TUC-09 無原局種子不得硬斷** — isolated annual action cannot manufacture a major event.
10. **TUC-10 反過度確定** — unresolved counterevidence blocks CERTAIN.
11. **TUC-11 十神 vs 宮位** — palace sets the domain; Ten God describes the function in that domain.
12. **TUC-12 做功效率 vs 字面吉凶** — effective functional chain outranks the pleasant/negative label of a Ten God.

## Source boundary
